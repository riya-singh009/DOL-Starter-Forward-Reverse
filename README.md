# Direct-On-Line (DOL) Starter – Forward & Reverse Control

## 📌 Project Overview
This project demonstrates the working of a **Direct-On-Line (DOL) starter** used for starting and controlling a **three-phase induction motor** with **forward and reverse rotation**.  
The circuit is designed to provide simple motor starting, direction control, and basic electrical safety using interlocking.

---

## ⚙️ Objective
- To understand the practical working of a DOL starter
- To control the **direction of rotation** (forward & reverse) of a three-phase induction motor
- To learn industrial motor control basics used in electrical panels and industries

---

## 🛠️ Components Used
- Three-phase induction motor  
- Main contactors (Forward & Reverse)  
- Overload relay  
- Push buttons (Start, Stop, Forward, Reverse)  
- Control supply  
- Wiring and protection components  

---

## 🔁 Working Principle
- When the **Forward Start** button is pressed, the forward contactor energizes and supplies power directly to the motor, allowing it to rotate in the forward direction.
- When the **Reverse Start** button is pressed, phase sequence is changed using the reverse contactor, causing the motor to rotate in the opposite direction.
- **Electrical interlocking** ensures that forward and reverse contactors cannot be energized simultaneously, preventing short circuits.
- The **overload relay** protects the motor from excessive current.

---

## 🔒 Safety Features
- Electrical interlocking between forward and reverse contactors
- Overload protection to prevent motor damage
- Manual stop control for emergency conditions
